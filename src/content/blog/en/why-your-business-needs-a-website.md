Many small businesses and entrepreneurs in Chile already have a presence on social media, and that's a good starting point. But social media and a website play different roles, and at Desarrolla Aysén we recommend thinking of them as complementary, not as a replacement for one another.

## Social media depends on someone else's platform

Your Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp Business profile doesn't belong to you: it belongs to the platform. The algorithm decides who sees your posts, the rules can change without notice, and if the account is lost or restricted, you lose your only digital presence overnight.

A website is yours. The domain, the design and the content are under your control, and don't depend on a third party's decisions.

## A website builds more trust

When a potential customer searches for your business and only finds a social media profile, it's easy for them to wonder: is this a real business? Is it still active? How do I contact them directly? A well-organized website, with your services, your contact info and good load speed, answers those questions in seconds.

## It helps people find you on Google

Social media posts rarely show up in search engines. A website, on the other hand, can be indexed by Google, giving you an additional — and more lasting — way for new customers to find you when they search for what you offer.

## It gives you a space to show everything you do

A social media post gives you limited space. A website lets you organize your services, explain your value proposition, showcase your portfolio and make it easy to get in touch, all in one place designed to turn visits into inquiries.

## It's not about choosing one or the other

We help our clients integrate both channels: a WhatsApp button or your social media linked from your site, and your site shared from your posts. Our goal is for your business to have a solid digital presence, without depending on a single channel.
